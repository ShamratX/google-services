Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Northline (“we,” “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit northline.example or submit a service contract request for Google Business Reviews, Google My Business (Google Business Profile setup), Google Ads Campaigns, or Web Development.

Who we are

Northline is a local digital services practice focused on Google Maps visibility, paid search, and owned websites for small businesses. Our contact email is hello@northline.example.

Information we collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through the service contract form on our homepage, including:

  • Client name
  • Business email address
  • Phone number
  • Company or business name
  • Public Google Maps listing URL (optional)
  • Selected service: Google Business Reviews, Google My Business, Google Ads Campaigns, or Web Development

We do not require you to create an account to browse the site. We do not run a checkout or payment form on this website.

How we use your information

We use contract form data only to:

  • Reply to your service inquiry with scope, timing, and next steps
  • Prepare a proposal for the Google Maps, Google Ads, or website work you selected
  • Follow up on an active contract discussion you started

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers, review sellers, or marketing lists.

Google Business Profile and Google Maps data

If you share a public Google Maps URL or ask us to work on your Google Business Profile, we may view publicly available listing information — business name, address or service area, categories, hours, photos, and reviews — to scope Google My Business setup, listing optimization, or Google Business Reviews work. Access to manage a listing stays in a Google account you own unless you explicitly invite Northline as a manager for ongoing edits.

Google Ads accounts

For Google Ads Campaigns, work is performed inside a Google Ads account you own. Google bills you directly for ad spend. Northline does not collect your payment card for Google media. We may need your business email and account access details only to build or restructure campaigns you have approved.

Website development and hosting

For Web Development projects, we may collect login details for your domain registrar, Cloudflare account, CMS, or hosting only as needed to launch a site you own. Our standard setup includes Cloudflare hosting and SSL. You pay the domain registrar separately. Source files and admin access are handed to you at project completion.

Cookies and analytics

This site is largely static. We do not use invasive tracking pixels or sell browsing behavior. Your browser may store standard technical data such as cache files. If we add analytics in the future, this policy will be updated.

Data retention

Contract inquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond, quote, deliver the service, or meet reasonable business record requirements. If you want a form submission deleted, email hello@northline.example from the same address you used on the form.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold from your contract form submission. You may opt out of follow-up emails at any time by replying “stop” or asking us to close the inquiry.

Children’s privacy

Our services are for business owners and operators. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when our services or data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the revision. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: hello@northline.example
