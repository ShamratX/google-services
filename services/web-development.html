Service 03

If you still do not have a website, every click dies in the listing.

Maps and ads can send people your way. Without a site, they get a phone number and a guess. We build custom-coded or CMS websites for businesses starting from zero — pages that load on a phone, say what you do, and let someone tap to call. You keep the keys. Hosting and SSL go on Cloudflare. You only pay for the domain.

Contract this service

Built for shops that skipped the internet

This is not a redesign of a ten-year-old brochure. It is a first site. The kind a tradesperson, clinic, or storefront needs when Google finally sends traffic and there is nowhere decent to land. We keep the page count honest: home, services, contact, and whatever proof you actually have.

Custom-coded when the layout should stay light and fast. A CMS when you need to edit hours, photos, or posts without calling us. Either way, the files and accounts are yours. We do not hold the site hostage behind a monthly “platform fee.”

What ships with the contract

  • A site that works in a parking lot. Large tap targets, click-to-call, readable type, and a layout that does not collapse on a cheap Android.
  • 100% full access ownership. Logins, DNS when you want it, and the codebase. If you leave, you take the site with you.
  • Cloudflare hosting and SSL, set up free. We put the site on Cloudflare and turn on HTTPS. There is no separate hosting invoice from Northline.
  • You pay the registrar for the domain. Namecheap, Google Domains successor, Cloudflare Registrar — your choice. That is the only name-related bill that sits with you.

How a typical engagement runs

  1. Step 1

    Scope the first version

    Pages, photos you already have, the offer, and whether custom code or a CMS is the saner path. We do not invent a twenty-page sitemap for a five-person shop.

  2. Step 2

    Build and put it live

    Design in the browser, connect the domain you registered, enable SSL on Cloudflare, and hand you the access list in writing.

  3. Step 3

    Point the rest of Google at it

    Website URL on the Maps listing. Landing page for ads if you also run paid search. The site is useless if nothing links to it.

Questions we hear before the contract

Do you keep admin after launch?+

Only if you ask us to stay on for edits. Default is full access in your name. That is the point of the ownership badge in the footer.

Can this wait until we have reviews?+

You can collect reviews with no site. Ads and Maps clicks convert better with one. If the listing is empty, add Maps review management.

What does “only pay for domain” cover?+

Hosting and the SSL certificate are handled through Cloudflare as part of our setup. The yearly domain renewal is yours with the registrar. The build itself is a separate project fee, agreed in the contract.
