Service 03 · Google Ads Campaigns

Pay for the search that already wants to hire you.

Google Ads is expensive when the account is a default campaign, a handful of broad keywords, and a homepage pretending to be a landing page. We build paid search around the phrases people type when they are ready to call — then we keep cutting the queries that never should have been billed.

Contract this service See the field work

What “high-converting” means here

It does not mean a vanity click-through rate. It means the people who tap the ad are close enough to buying that your calendar or inbox actually moves. For a plumber that is “emergency leak” in the right zip codes. For a clinic it is “accepting new patients near me,” not a lecture about wellness.

We write ads that sound like the business, not like a template factory. Extensions, call assets, and location targeting are part of the build, not leftovers. Tracking is set so you can see calls and form fills — not just sessions.

Inside the campaign build

  • Account structure. Separate the searches that mean “buy now” from the ones that mean “just looking.” Mixing them is how budgets disappear.
  • Negatives from day one. Job seekers, DIY queries, and out-of-area terms get blocked before they eat the first week of spend.
  • Landing that matches the promise. If you already have a site, we aim traffic at a page that can close. If you do not, that is a web development conversation first.
  • Your ads account, your media bill. Google charges you for clicks. Northline charges for setup and management. Those invoices stay separate on purpose.

A note on “guaranteed sales”

Nobody honest can promise a fixed number of closed jobs from paid search. What we can do is aim the account at demand that already exists, measure the leads, and stop paying for traffic that never had a chance. If your offer, area, or follow-up is broken, ads will show that quickly — which is still useful.

How a typical engagement runs

  1. Step 1

    Map the money searches

    We list the phrases that already imply a job, the radius you can serve, and the budget you can stand for thirty days without panic.

  2. Step 2

    Launch a tight account

    Campaigns, ads, and conversion actions go live in your Google Ads account. You keep admin access. Nothing is locked in an agency black box.

  3. Step 3

    Trim what does not pay

    Search terms, locations, and schedules get edited against real leads — not a dashboard that only celebrates clicks.

Questions we hear before the contract

Do I need a website to run ads?+

Call-only campaigns can work for some trades. Most offers still need a page people can trust after the click. If you have no site, start with web development.

What if my Maps listing has no reviews?+

Ads can still run. Conversion usually suffers when the map pack looks empty or unclaimed. Pair this with Google Business Reviews for stars, or Google Business Profile setup if the listing itself is wrong.

Who pays Google?+

You do, inside your own ads account. Our fee is for the build and the ongoing edits. Media spend is never marked up through Northline.
