What Google Business Profile work actually is

A Google Business Profile is the card in Google Search and Google Maps when someone looks for a plumber, HVAC contractor, electrician, restaurant, salon, or any other local business near them. If there is no profile, you do not appear. If the profile is unclaimed, Google is guessing. If it is claimed and still thin, the map pack shows the wrong category and a photo that does not look like the job.

Northline builds new listings and optimizes existing ones. Primary category, secondary categories, name-address-phone that matches the van or the door, hours, the services you actually sell, attributes, the website field, Q&A, and photos from the real work. Access lives in a Google account you own. We do not keyword-stuff the business name. We do not buy reviews as part of this contract.

If the pin is already accurate and the problem is silence under Reviews, open Google Business Reviews instead. Most businesses eventually need both. They are not the same job.

This work is for you if

  • You have no Google Maps listing yet and need a new profile created.
  • You have a listing and it is unclaimed, incomplete, or held by an old vendor.
  • Hours, category, services, or phone number do not match the business.
  • Google shows two pins, or photos that are not your jobs.
  • You are a storefront, a salon, a restaurant, or a service-area trade that should not publish a home address.

What we complete on the listing

Every field below is public. Google uses it to decide which searches you belong in. We write it the way the business actually operates — not the way a template guesses.

Claim, verify, and ownership

We search Maps first so we do not create a second listing. Then we claim or request access, walk verification in your name — video, postcard, phone, or email, whichever Google offers — and leave owner access on an account you control.

Categories, services, and NAP

One honest primary category, useful secondary categories, a service list people actually buy, and name, address, phone, and website that match the door, the invoice, and the site. Keyword stuffing in the title is how listings get suspended.

Photos, hours, and posts

Cover, logo, interior, team, products, and job-site stills that look like the work. Regular hours plus holiday exceptions. Occasional posts so the profile does not freeze on a 2019 grand opening.

Q&A, products, and duplicates

Public questions get answers a stranger can use. Menus or products go on when they belong. Duplicate or spam listings get reported. The map should show one business, not two ghosts.

How new setup and existing optimization run

  1. Step 1

    Find or create the pin

    You send a Maps URL if you have one. If you do not, we search first so we do not duplicate. No listing means we start a new Google Business Profile. An existing one gets an audit of claim status, categories, hours, photos, and NAP.

  2. Step 2

    Claim and verify

    Access in your name. Video walkthrough of the location or branded van when Google asks for it. Postcard or phone when that is what they offer. No contractor Gmail left holding the keys.

  3. Step 3

    Build or optimize the card

    Categories, the real service list, photos from jobs or the floor, hours, website, attributes, and Q&A. We write what a stranger sees in the map pack before they dial.

  4. Step 4

    Hand you a live listing

    You keep owner. We can stay on as manager. If you also need reviews, ads, or a site URL to put on the profile, those are separate pages.

Google Business Profile setup questions

Do you only work with shops?+

No. We set up new Google Business Profiles and optimize existing Google Maps listings for any local business — trades, restaurants, salons, and the rest. If you need a Maps profile, we take the contract.

Is this new setup, or only fixing a listing I already have?+

Both. If there is no pin, we create and verify a new Google Business Profile. If you already have one, we optimize categories, services, photos, hours, NAP, and duplicates. Send the Maps URL if you have it, or say there is none.

Is Google My Business the same as Google Business Profile?+

Yes. Owners still say Google My Business. Google renamed the product to Google Business Profile. The listing on Maps and Search is the same pin.

Is this the same as Google Business Reviews?+

No. Google Business Profile setup and optimization is the listing: claim, verify, categories, photos, hours, posts. Review management is how real customers leave Google reviews. Most local businesses eventually need both.

Do you keep the Google login?+

No. The Business Profile stays in an account you own. We can be invited as a manager if you want ongoing edits. Default is your name on the door.

Do you buy Google reviews with this setup?+

No. Finishing the profile does not include purchased stars, fake check-ins, or spam posts. Google suspends listings for that. If you need authentic reviews from real customers, that is Google Reviews.

Can you set up a service-area business with no storefront?+

Yes. Trades and mobile operators can hide the street address and show a service area instead. Verification still has to prove the business is real. We set the pin the way Google allows for that category.

Will this replace Google Ads?+

A clean profile helps paid clicks convert. It does not buy the search. If you need inbound this month, see Google Ads. If clicks have nowhere to land, look at web development.
