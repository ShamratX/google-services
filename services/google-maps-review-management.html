Service 01

A Google pin with no reviews is a closed sign nobody bothers to read.

Plenty of local businesses already have a Maps listing. Hours are set. The address is right. And still the review count sits at zero. Strangers treat that silence as a warning. This page is the work we do to change that — with feedback from people who already used you, not a purchased pile of stars.

Contract this service

Who this is for

You show up on Google Maps. Maybe you claimed the profile years ago and then left it alone. Maybe a customer tried to leave a review and got lost. Either way, the listing looks unfinished. Competitors with thirty ordinary reviews keep winning the tap, even when your work is better.

We step in when the problem is social proof, not a missing pin. If you do not have a listing yet, we still want the public Maps link once it exists — the form on the contract page has a field for it.

What the work actually includes

  • Profile cleanup. Categories, services, photos, and hours that match how you really operate — so new reviews attach to a listing that looks alive.
  • A request flow that feels human. Timing after a good visit, language your staff can actually say, and a path that lands on Google’s own review screen. No fake portals.
  • Staff habits, not a one-week burst. The listing keeps growing after the setup week because asking becomes part of closing the job, not a marketing stunt.
  • Local search that can use the signal. Fresh, authentic reviews are one of the few public proofs Google still leans on for map pack ranking. Empty profiles give it nothing to work with.

What we will not do

We do not buy Google reviews, run review farms, or write them for people who never visited. That kind of inventory gets listings filtered or suspended, and it is not the contract. If a competitor offered you “50 five-stars next week,” that is not this service.

How a typical engagement runs

  1. Step 1

    Read the listing

    You send the public Google Maps link. We look at what is missing, what is wrong, and whether Google already treats the profile as established or brand new.

  2. Step 2

    Install the ask

    QR, SMS, email, or a line at the register — whichever matches how you actually finish a job. The goal is one honest request while the visit is still warm.

  3. Step 3

    Keep it honest

    We watch for filtered posts, reply guidance, and photo updates so the profile does not go quiet again after the first handful of reviews.

Questions we hear before the contract

Can you guarantee a star rating?+

No. Real customers write what they experienced. We can raise the number of genuine reviews and clean the listing so those posts are visible. We cannot script five stars.

Do we need a website first?+

Not for this service. Maps can send calls on its own. If clicks have nowhere to land, look at web development as a second contract.

Will this replace Google Ads?+

Reviews make paid traffic convert better. They do not buy the click. If you need inbound this month, open the Google Ads page.
