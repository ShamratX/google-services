FAQ

Questions we hear before the contract

Straight answers. If your question is about one service only, the page for that service has more.

Do you buy Google reviews?+

No. We never buy stars, farm accounts, or post fake check-ins. Review work is a timed ask after a real job so customers who already used you can write what they experienced. That is Google Business Reviews.

Is Google My Business the same as Google Business Reviews?+

No. Google My Business is the listing: claim, verify, hours, photos, categories. Review management is how real customers leave Google reviews. Most local businesses eventually need both. They are separate contracts. Start with Google My Business if the pin is missing or thin.

Who owns the website and the Google login?+

You do. The Business Profile stays in an account in your name. Website source files are yours. Cloudflare hosting and SSL are set up at no extra charge. You pay only for the domain. See web development if you need the site from zero.

Who pays for Google Ads clicks?+

You pay Google for media. Northline builds and runs the campaigns. We do not mark up a mystery ad budget as a product. The invoice from Google is yours to see. Detail lives on the Google Ads page.
