Privacy

Northline collects only what you type into the service contract form: name, email, phone, business name, optional Maps URL, and the service you selected. That data is used to reply to your request and to prepare a working agreement. It is not sold, rented, or dropped into a third-party ad audience.

Form submissions are stored in your browser so you can see a confirmation, and a draft email may open on your device so the request can actually reach us. We do not run hidden trackers on this page beyond what your own browser or network may add.

If you want a record removed, email hello@northline.example with the address you used. We will delete what we hold.

Terms

Submitting this form requests a conversation. It does not create a paid contract until both sides agree in writing on scope, price, and timeline. Review work covers legitimate collection of feedback from real customers. Purchased or incentivized fake Google reviews are outside the agreement and will not be performed.

Web projects include full access and ownership of the finished site. Cloudflare hosting and SSL are set up at no extra fee from Northline. Domain registration is paid by the client to the registrar of their choice. Ads spend is billed by Google to the client’s ads account; Northline charges for setup and management, not for Google’s media cost.

These terms can be replaced by a signed service contract when work begins. If the two differ, the signed contract wins.
